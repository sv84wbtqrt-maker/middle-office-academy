# Roadmap: from finance basics to middle office analyst in energy trading

Target job: middle office analyst in the trading arm of an integrated energy major such as
TotalEnergies (Trading & Shipping: crude oil and refined products, mainly from Geneva; gas, LNG and
power, mainly from London). Public facts only: the course never claims to know internal
TotalEnergies systems, team names or figures.

## How the course works
- One lesson per day, copied by hand in 10 to 15 minutes (about 180 to 300 words to write).
- A new lesson only after the previous one is marked "written".
- **Every 7th lesson (7, 14, 21, ...) is a review lesson**: no new concept, a short recap of the
  last six lessons, a mini case mixing them, and questions. It is the spaced repetition of the course.
- **The learner's questions come first**: a question sent from the website becomes the next
  lesson (or part of it) within 3 lessons at most, linked into the story.
- Modules are a guide, not a cage: the teacher can reorder, merge or add lessons when the learner's
  feedback or questions call for it. Lesson numbers below are estimates.

## The running story (keeps the course logical)
The learner is "you", a new middle office analyst at **Northbridge Energy Trading**, a fictional
trading desk of a large energy group. Recurring characters (all fictional, no gendered pronouns
needed): **Sam** (head of middle office, your mentor), **Chris** (crude oil trader), **Dana**
(gas and power trader), **Lee** (back office / operations), **Robin** (market risk manager).
Recurring trade objects, introduced in module 2 and followed until the end:
- **Cargo NS-01**: a cargo of 600,000 barrels of North Sea crude bought by Chris, then hedged.
- **TTF-Q1**: a gas position on the Dutch TTF hub for next winter, traded by Dana.
- Later: a power spread, an LNG cargo, an option, a trade error, a limit breach, an audit.
Each object comes back in later lessons (P&L, risk, settlement, accounting, controls), so the
learner sees the full life of real trades.
